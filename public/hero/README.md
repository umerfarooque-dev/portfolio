# Hero slides

Drop five images here, named:

    liquid-slide-1.jpg
    liquid-slide-2.jpg
    liquid-slide-3.jpg
    liquid-slide-4.jpg
    liquid-slide-5.jpg

Landscape, at least 2400px wide, dark/abstract works best (the headline sits
on top of them behind a scrim).

They are picked up automatically by `HeroSection` -> `HeroSlideshow`. Until
they exist the hero renders a plain dark field, so nothing breaks.

To change the filenames or count, edit `SLIDES` in
`src/components/organisms/HeroSection.tsx`.
